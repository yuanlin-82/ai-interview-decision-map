## Why this page exists

- **Scan** differences across types without opening eleven maps.  
- **Handoff:** engineers/PMs see pack families + don’ts + where the diagram lives.  
- **Eval:** score “wrong family for this type” as a route fail even when prose is fluent.

Spoken realizations stay private.
