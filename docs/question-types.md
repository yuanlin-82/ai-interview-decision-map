# Question-Type Matrix

Intent-level only. No spoken examples.

This is an **illustrative subset** of follow-up packs — enough to show how typed strategies hang off one shared router. It is **not** an exhaustive product catalog.

Each type shares the **overview router** (abnormal vs normal).  
Differences live in the **normal strategy pack** and optional **type-specific boundary** notes.

| Type key | Assessment goal | Normal strategy pack (pick ONE probe family / turn) | Boundary notes |
|----------|-----------------|------------------------------------------------------|----------------|
| `behavioral` | Past behavior as evidence of future performance | Ground abstract claims in a real episode; or deepen a concrete episode (clarity / judgment / contribution / reflection) | — |
| `situational` | Capability under scenario tension | Past-event deepen under constraints; or structured hypothetical plan / trade-off / risk — avoid endless failure chains | — |
| `career-choice` | Decision process behind career direction | Motivation & self-awareness; or preparation & actionability of the choice | — |
| `job-transition` | Drivers of job moves (non-judgmental) | Decision process; values; patterns across moves | Deflect employer-bashing into environment-fit without moralizing |
| `opening-intro` | Opening narrative: claims, motivation, clarity | Verify a claim; probe motivation; distill fit signal | Thin intros → one ask for role-relevant experience |
| `fallback` | **Safe continuation** when no specialized pack fits | One clarifying deepen (e.g. a single 5W1H cell) **or** one example request — keep the interview moving without forcing a wrong competency frame | Prefer continuity and neutrality over “clever” probing |

## How to use this table

- **Product / research:** compare types by *goal* and *probe family*, not by copy.  
- **Implementation:** map each probe family to private templates.  
- **Eval:** score whether the chosen family matched the contract, independent of fluency.
