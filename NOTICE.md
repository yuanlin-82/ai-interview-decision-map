# Notice

## What this repository is

This repository documents a **decision contract** for AI-led interview follow-up turns: how an interviewer agent should route after hearing a candidate, reverse-derived from real multi-turn interview dialogues.

## What this repository is not

It does **not** include:

- Production system prompts (any language or model vendor)
- Fixed spoken utterance libraries used in production
- Raw interview transcripts, ASR dumps, or personal data
- Deployable configs, variable IDs, or internal tooling bindings

## Intellectual property

The **methodology, decision maps, and design principles** in this repository are shared for research and industry discussion.

**Production prompts, copy decks, and operational implementations remain proprietary** and are intentionally withheld to protect intellectual property and candidate privacy.

## Data ethics

Walkthroughs are **synthetic or fully anonymized composites** patterned after observed decision paths. They are not real candidate records.
